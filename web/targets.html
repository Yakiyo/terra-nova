Choose a target site

Each site is described by the conditions NASA missions measured there. TerraNova then ranks every land cell on Earth by how closely it matches.

Target values cite measurements from LRO (Diviner, LOLA), Chang'e-2, Mars Express (HRSC), Mars Global Surveyor (MOLA), MRO (HiRISE), Curiosity and Perseverance, plus NASA fact sheets. Every value lists its source in the Finder.
