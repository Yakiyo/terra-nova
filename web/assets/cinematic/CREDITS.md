# Planetary imagery

Source: Solar System Scope / INOVE, https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Downloaded 2026-11-05. Local derivatives resize the original 8K Earth day map,
Earth clouds, Moon, and Mars textures to 4096×2048 and recompress them as JPEG.
Milky Way background is the 2K stars/Milky Way map. These are decorative planetary
maps, not measurements or site-level imagery. Solar System Scope describes its
textures as NASA-derived and color-adjusted, with some unmapped planetary areas
filled with representative terrain.
